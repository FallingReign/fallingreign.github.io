Descale

Preventive descale · allow about 2 hours, including the soak

Why the boilers never “empty”

Hot water comes out of the steam boiler. The custom cup button runs water through the brew boiler to the group. Whatever you take out, the pump tops back up from the glass tank, so you can’t drain them, and you don’t need to. You’re pushing the old water out by pulling new water through. The German manual says the same: press the hot water button and keep drawing until the tank is empty. This guide stops just before it’s empty, so the pump never runs dry.

Preventive only. If the machine is already heavily scaled (slow flow, grit coming out), have a dealer descale it. Loose scale can block the lines.
Golden rule: watch the glass tank. The Bellona has no low-water warning, so keep the tank where you can see it, not behind the machine, and never let it run empty while the pump runs.

If the pump runs dry

The tank is down to the end of the tube, the pump turns loud and rattly, and water sputters or stops.

  1. Stop the pump right away: let go of the button, or press it again if it keeps running. If it still runs, turn the machine off.
  2. Refill the tank.
  3. Press hot water until the flow runs smooth and steady again.

Be quick: the longer the pump runs dry, the more it risks damage.

Clean inside the steam wand

About once a week if you steam milk most days, or when the steam turns weak · about 15 minutes plus the soak

How milk gets inside

When you push the steam lever up with the tip still in the milk, the steam left in the wand cools and pulls a little milk up inside. That’s why the manual has you wipe the wand and give it a quick blast after every jug. What stays behind dries into a film no cloth can reach. This clean uses that pull on purpose: short blasts in a jug of cleaner pull it up inside and push it out again, then it soaks and gets rinsed the same way.

Every time you steam (from the manual): wipe the wand with a damp cloth straight away, then open the lever briefly to blow out what got pulled inside. This clean is for what still builds up.
Hot. The wand, its tip and the steam can burn. Work the lever, not the wand, and hold the jug by its handle.

If a hole in the tip is blocked

The steam stays weak or uneven even once the machine is fully hot.

  1. Push the lever up, switch off, unplug and let the machine cool.
  2. Unscrew the steam tip and soak it in the cleaner.
  3. Push a pin gently through each hole, without widening it. Rinse the tip.
  4. Screw it back on snugly, heat up and check it doesn’t leak.

This is the manual’s fix for weak steam: unscrew the steam nozzle and clean or replace it.

From the Bellezza manual: wipe the wand and blow it out after every jug, blow out the water before each jug, and if the steam is weak once the machine is hot, unscrew the steam nozzle to clean or replace it. The pulses are Puly Milk’s steam wand method: 1 second on, 10 seconds off, 7 to 10 times in the cleaner, then the same in clean water. Soak times are the cleaner makers’ (Cafetto MFC Green: 5 minutes, Urnex Rinza: 15 to 30). The once a week rhythm is common practice, not Bellezza’s. Animations are sped up.

Clean the group head

About every 50 shots, as a dealer suggests · about 15 minutes, with the heat-up

How a backflush cleans it

Coffee oil and fine grounds build up on the shower screen, around the seal and in the valve behind them, and go stale and bitter. With the blind filter in, water can’t get through, so pressure builds in the group. When you let go, the Bellona’s valve dumps that water back out into the drip tray, taking the oil with it. It’s the same drain you see when descaling.

You’ll need

  • Espresso machine cleanerBackflush powder, such as Cafetto Espresso Clean, Urnex Cafiza or Puly Caff. Not descaler.
  • Blind filterThe cleaning cover from the box, in the double basket, or a solid blind basket.
  • BrushThe cleaning brush that came with the machine.
  • MugA big mug that fits under the group.
Every day (from the manual): after your last coffee, knock out the portafilter, rinse it under hot water and leave it out of the group, so the seal isn’t squeezed and the screen can dry.
Now and then (a dealer’s tip): with the machine off and cool, unscrew the shower screen (one screw) and soak it overnight in the cleaner.

From the Bellezza manual: brush the shower screen with the brush from the box and rinse it with the custom cup button, and after your last coffee rinse the portafilter and leave it out of the group. Backflushing suits the Bellona: its parts list has the three-way valve that drains the group into the tray, and its dealers say to backflush it (1st-line: about every 50 shots, Di Pacci: regularly). The dose and the short presses follow 1st-line’s routine for vibration pump machines like this one, with a second or two added for the Bellona’s pre-infusion pause. Animations are sped up.
